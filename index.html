RecastEngine

The engine is the reusable part of SciRecast. It reads the source, writes the modern version, compiles the untouched original as the reference, and refuses to call the result correct unless a gate says so. It knows nothing about any particular model: domain knowledge attaches through plugins, and the engine passing its tests with every domain package uninstalled is a standing check.

New here? Getting started takes you from an empty machine to a verified translation of your own module in about twenty minutes. This chapter is the map.

CESM Case

The first model the engine is used on: the Community Earth System Model. Two atmosphere pipelines and a shared validation layer. Every component is its own repository.

Pipeline 1Pipeline 2
ModelCAM5CAM6
TargetRewrite to Python and decouple the physics into modulesRewrite to JAX/Numba/CUDA, GPU-accelerated kernels

Validation and securityCC-Test, the gate for both Correctness and Cyber testing;
Sec-Track (restricted), the repo of identified vulnerabilities.

A reproduced case. The multilayer canopy of the Community Land Model (CLM-ml v2, 76 Fortran modules) was taken to NumPy and JAX under the engine and a small domain extension, reproducing arXiv:2606.07681.
